Good morning students. Today we explore chapter one of insert class 10 mathematics, real numbers. Our primary foundation is the fundamental theorem of arithmetic. The theorem states that every composite number can be expressed uniquely as a product of prime numbers, apart from the order in which the prime factors occur. For example, let us factorise 36. 36 is 2 times 2 times 3 times 3, which is 2 squared times 3 squared. We also use this method to compute the highest common factor, or HCF, and the least common multiple, or LCM. The HCF is the product of the smallest powers of each common prime factor involved in the numbers. The LCM is the product of the greatest powers of each prime factor involved in the numbers. An essential formula to remember is, for any two positive integers A and B, the product of HCF of A and B and LCM of A and B is equal to the product of A and B. Finally, we can rigorously prove that square root of 2 and square root of 3 are irrational numbers using the method of contradiction. Review these core theorems and practice exercise 1.1.
